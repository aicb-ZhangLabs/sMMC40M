# Data available on Hugging Face

All 124 acquisitions and the benchmark assets can now be downloaded from
[zhang-lab/sMCC40M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhang-lab/sMCC40M), one sample at a time or in full.

<!-- more -->

- Per-sample packages with expression, image, mask, metadata, mappings, eligibility and checksums.
- A `download_sample.py` helper with examples for HD-002 (Visium HD) and HHDX011 (Xenium).
- The frozen benchmark directory and per-sample licensing and attribution notices.

[How to download](../../dataset/download.md)
