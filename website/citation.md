# Cite

If you use sMCC40M, please cite the NeurIPS 2026 paper:

```bibtex
@inproceedings{li2026smcc40m,
  title     = {sMCC40M: A Context-Aware Dataset and Benchmark for Single-Cell Spatial Transcriptomics},
  author    = {Li, Xi and Hu, Yaqi and Duan, Ziheng and Wang, Xinyi and Sun, Simon Dongbo and
               Sikdar, Diptanshu and Liu, Yang and Zhang, Jing},
  booktitle = {Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems},
  year      = {2026}
}
```

When you share data from the release, keep the per-sample attribution notices
([license manifest](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhang-lab/sMCC40M/blob/main/licensing/license_manifest.csv)).

## Contact

Questions, corrections or reproducibility reports: open an
[issue](https://github.com/aicb-ZhangLabs/sMMC40M/issues) with the release version,
protocol track and affected sample IDs.
